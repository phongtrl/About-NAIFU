A little about me

I'm Phong — better known online as NAIFU ナイフ. What started as sharpening a single dull kitchen knife at home turned into a real obsession with bringing rusted, chipped, forgotten blades back to life.

I love the quiet focus of it — reading the bevel, feeling the burr, and chasing that perfect, hair-splitting edge. Every step gets filmed and shared with a community that loves a sharp edge as much as I do.
