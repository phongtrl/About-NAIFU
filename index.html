Hi, I'm Phong Vuoc Tran

Better known online as NAIFU ナイフ, I turned a simple fascination with sharp edges into a full-blown craft. What started as sharpening kitchen knives at home has grown into a passion for restoring rusted, chipped, and forgotten blades back to life.

Every knife tells a story. My job is to listen to that story through the steel — reading the bevel, feeling the burr, and chasing that perfect, hair-splitting edge. I share every step of that process with a growing community on TikTok.

Countless hours studying angles, grits, and Japanese sharpening traditions. Failure after failure taught me the feel of true steel.
