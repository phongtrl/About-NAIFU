A little about me

I'm Phong — better known online as NAIFU ナイフ. Ever since I was a kid I'd grab any rock or "stoney" thing I could find to sharpen my tools. It was never a real success — until 2019, when my first cheap whetstone made everything click and my skills rocketed.

By 2020 I turned that obsession into a service for professional chefs and home cooks, running my own knife-sharpening company from 2020 to 2025 and selling knives and kitchen tools along the way.

Today I focus on my engineering career — I hold a Master's and Bachelor's in Geography, Geomatics & Spatial Planning from NTNU Trondheim — while still sharing the quiet focus of the craft with my community on TikTok.
